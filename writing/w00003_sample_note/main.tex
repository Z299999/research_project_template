\begin{abstract}
This short note is a worked sample of the repository's writing workflow. For the
scalar damped harmonic oscillator, the damping ratio $\zeta$ alone determines
whether the response oscillates. We state the three-regime behaviour and
illustrate the underdamped case with a figure generated by the companion
experiment \texttt{e00001\_damped\_oscillator}. The note exists to demonstrate
structure, not to contribute a new result.
\end{abstract}
